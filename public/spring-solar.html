Why spring

The best time to clean is right before your panels work hardest.

01

Pollen season

September to November coats roofs across the Basin in a fine yellow film. You can't see it from the ground, but your inverter can.

02

Southeaster dust

Once the summer wind starts, dust and sea salt stick to that film. Starting summer with clean glass means less build-up.

03

Peak sun hours

December and January are your biggest production months. Clean panels then pay you back the fastest.
